Ms Qiu's AI Coding: Project 3 Submission
By Jayden Chan

Hallowtale is a haunted-maze-AI game that uses a top-down perspective.
In the game, the player controls a heart and defeats the boss and it's mini bosses.
Players navigate through a field filled with items, and are required to damage the boss by collecting these items.
The field is the home of the bosses, all who use first-order Markov models and A* pathfinding algorithms to attack the player.
Choices made by the player determine whether or not Halloween will marked a slaughter or a victory.
